Comparing quotes

From the Minnesota Department of Health.

  • The installer should visit the house before quoting, not quote by phone or email.
  • Ask for the individual’s license number and verify it.
  • Is the price firm? Are electrical work and permits included?
  • Do they guarantee results below 4.0 pCi/L, and what happens if the system misses?
  • Don’t pay in full until a post-mitigation test shows reduced levels.
  • Be wary of quotes under $1,000.

FAQ

What is the average cost of radon mitigation?

Angi’s 2026 data, from 1,184 projects, gives $805–$1,326 with an average of $1,043. HomeAdvisor’s 2026 page gives $786–$1,274 with an average of $1,028.

Is a very cheap quote a red flag?

The Minnesota Department of Health advises avoiding unlicensed installers and rates under $1,000, which it calls often too good to be true.
