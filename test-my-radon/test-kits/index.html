Radon test kits

EPA describes two types of tests: short-term (2–90 days, often charcoal canisters) and long-term (more than 90 days; more likely to reflect your year-round average). Source: EPA A Citizen’s Guide to Radon.

Many state radon programs offer free or discounted kits. Check yours via EPA’s state program list.
