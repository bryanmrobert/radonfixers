Does Radon Mitigation Work?

Yes. EPA says radon reduction systems work, and some can reduce radon levels in a home by up to 99%. Even very high levels can be brought down to acceptable levels. Maine CDC says any home can be fixed. Always retest after installation to confirm the result, and retest about every two years.

Most soil suction systems include a monitor that shows whether the system is running. A Massachusetts DPH checklist suggests getting a contract that guarantees levels stay below 4.0 pCi/L, ideally 2.0. University of Georgia Extension says systems typically last 10 to 15 years.
