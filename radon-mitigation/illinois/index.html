FAQ

How do I check that a radon mitigator is licensed in Illinois?

Look them up on the official Illinois Emergency Management Agency (IEMA-OHS) list linked on this page, and ask for their license or certification number before you sign anything.

How much does radon mitigation cost?

Angi’s 2026 data puts most active soil depressurization installs at $805–$1,326 (average $1,043). HomeAdvisor reports $786–$1,274 (average $1,028). Local quotes vary, so see our cost guide.

Does RadonFixers install radon systems?

No. RadonFixers does not perform installs. We list independent, state-licensed contractors and can pass your request to at most 3 of them.
